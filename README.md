# 🏥 AarogyaSeva AI – Rural Health Assistant

**An AI-powered rural healthcare companion for underserved communities.**

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://aarogya-seva-ai.vercel.app/

## 📌 About the Project

AarogyaSeva AI is a digital healthcare platform designed to help people in rural and underserved areas access essential healthcare information and services.

The platform provides a simple, accessible interface for discovering health information, finding nearby hospitals, understanding government healthcare schemes, and interacting with an AI-powered health assistant.

## ✨ Features

* 🤖 **AI Health Assistant** – Interactive healthcare guidance
* 🏥 **Hospital Locator** – Find healthcare facilities
* 📚 **Health Education** – Easy-to-understand health information
* 🏛️ **Government Scheme Finder** – Discover relevant healthcare schemes
* 🔔 **Health Reminders** – Manage important health reminders
* 🌐 **Multi-language Support** – Designed for accessibility
* 🎤 **Voice Interaction** – Voice-based assistance
* 🚨 **Emergency Support** – Quick access to emergency assistance
* 👩‍⚕️ **Doctor Companion** – Interactive healthcare companion
